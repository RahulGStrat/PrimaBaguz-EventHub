THE CULINARY ARENA

Battle of The Chefs is a premier culinary competition that brings together talented chefs to compete in high-pressure, live cooking challenges designed to test precision, innovation, presentation, and technical expertise.

More than a competition, the event serves as a platform for culinary professionals to exchange ideas, elevate industry standards, and inspire the next generation of food innovation.

As proud sponsors, PrimaBaguz & Gourmessa are committed to supporting chefs by providing quality products and culinary solutions that help transform creative ideas into exceptional dining experiences.

Together, we celebrate the passion, craftsmanship, and innovation that continue to shape the future of the foodservice industry.

COMPETITION CATEGORIES

CLASS M41
Team of 2 Chefs
Charcuterie Table for Seven (7)

GOURMESSA CHARCUTERIE
SIGNATURE CHALLENGE

Time Limit: 120 Minutes (2 Hours)

Requirement: Charcuterie table for seven (7) pax on 90cm x 90cm table with white cloth.

MANDATORY PRODUCT LIST

  • Bangi Chicken Frankfurter 40gm (8 pcs) 320g
  • Cheese & Chicken Sausage 80gm (5 pcs) 400g
  • Chicken Mosaic (Pre-Sliced) 200g
  • Chicken Delight Square (Pre-Sliced) 200g
  • Smoked Duck Breast (Whole) 200g
  • Beef Pepperoni (Pre-Sliced) 200g
  • Beef Salami (Pre-Sliced) 200g
  • Roast Beef (Pre-Sliced) 200g

TABLE MUST CONSIST OF :

  • 2 Types of dips, condiments, crackers
  • 3 Types of Cheeses
  • 1 Meatloaf with Condiments & Sauces
  • 1 Terrine (hot or cold)
  • 7 Mini burgers (Raw meat Patty CAN be brought in)
  • 2 Types of sandwiches (finger, open faced or gourmet)
  • Judges will judge the charcuterie table & select the items for tasting.
  • Recipes & Menu are required.
  • NO Pork & Pork Items are allowed to be used for all dishes.
* Limited to 30 teams only.
CLASS M50
Individual
Sandwich Toast Master

MERRYCHEF PRIMABAGUZ
SANDWICH TOAST MASTER

Time Limit: 20 Minutes

Requirement: Prepare and present two (2) sets, individual plate
of sandwich.

MANDATORY PRODUCT LIST

  • Chicken Cold Cuts (Pre-Sliced) - 100g
  • Beef Streaky (Pre-Sliced) - 100g

EQUIPMENT

  • MUST use MERRYCHEF Speed Oven for final cooking/reheat.
  • ONE (1) set for judging & ONE (1) set for display
  • Participants must use at least one or both GOURMESSA products co-sponsor by PrimaBaguz
  • The plate must include any combination of condiments, appropriate garnishes & sauces.
  • NO metal plates are allowed into the Merrychef speed oven.
* Limited to 20 Participants only.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?

Anyone who loves cooking! Our events are open to amateur chefs, home cooks, and food enthusiasts of all skill levels.

WHAT INGREDIENTS ARE PROVIDED?

We provide all the fresh, high-quality base ingredients needed for the chosen recipes, including proteins, fresh vegetables, herbs, and spices.

CAN I BRING MY OWN INGREDIENTS?

To ensure fairness and food safety compliance, we ask that you only use the ingredients provided by our kitchen staff during the event.
